# Sources and Method Basis

This skill is an original synthesis and does not reproduce proprietary standard text.

## Official / industry sources

1. Eclipse Capella / Arcadia
   - https://projects.eclipse.org/projects/polarsys.capella
   - https://www.eclipse.org/community/eclipse_newsletter/2017/december/article3.php
   - Influence: separation of operational need, functional behavior, logical architecture and physical hosting.

2. Automotive SPICE 4.0, VDA QMC
   - https://vda-qmc.de/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/AutomotiveSPICE_PAM_40_Chinese.pdf
   - Influence: SYS.3 static/dynamic system architecture, architecture analysis/rationale, consistency and bidirectional traceability.

3. Siemens Capital Systems Architect
   - https://www.siemens.com/en-us/products/capital/offerings/systems-architect/
   - Influence: rules/constraints-based allocation of functions/signals to resources/carriers and architecture optimization.

4. AUTOSAR
   - https://www.autosar.org/standards
   - https://www.autosar.org/standards/adaptive-platform
   - Influence: ECU/system topology, software-to-ECU mapping, communication and service/API-oriented high-performance ECU architecture.

5. Bosch E/E Architecture
   - https://www.bosch-mobility.com/en/mobility-topics/ee-architecture/
   - https://www.bosch-mobility.com/en/solutions/control-units/zone-ecu/
   - Influence: vehicle-centralized, zone-oriented architecture and separation of vehicle computer, ZCU, embedded ECU, sensors and actuators.

6. OMG SysML
   - https://www.omg.org/spec/SysML/2.0
   - Influence: systems modeling and traceable architecture views.
